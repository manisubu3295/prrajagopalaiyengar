# Audit Summary

## What exists

- The site already has strong call and WhatsApp conversion points.
- The site already includes clear route-based product sections for electrical, plumbing, pumps and borewell enquiries.
- Mobile sticky actions already exist through the floating WhatsApp button and sticky call bar.
- Core local business metadata, canonical handling and FAQ schema already exist.

## What was missing

- Authorized Texmo Motor Dealer positioning was not prominent in the hero, trust messaging, footer or SEO.
- Tamil support was not implemented with correct web fonts or visible bilingual usage.
- Brand priority logic did not yet make Texmo, Philips, RR, KEI, Legrand and Finolex sufficiently prominent together.
- The design still leaned more decorative than a premium local dealer showroom style.
- About messaging did not yet emphasize 67 years of service across 3 generations.

## What was wrong

- Texmo authority, which is the strongest business signal, was underrepresented.
- Tamil rendering support was missing even though the website needs a bilingual feel.
- Some earlier content still read like internal strategy copy rather than customer-facing website copy.
- Homepage brand emphasis did not fully reflect the exact business positioning required for local dominance.

# Critical Missing Issues

- Texmo positioning must be visible in hero, trust, footer and SEO.
- Tamil font support must use Noto Sans Tamil with safe fallbacks.
- Homepage must instantly communicate Tiruvarur, Thiruthuraipoondi and Mannargudi service coverage.
- Pumps and motors section must clearly connect to Texmo dealership authority.
- About section must avoid founder details while still using the 67-year, 3-generation trust signal.
